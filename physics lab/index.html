From the question to the answer

Horizontal and vertical motion happen independently. Gravity changes only the particle’s vertical velocity.

01 Given

Horizontal velocity, u
2.0 m/s
Tower height, h
10.0 m
Gravity, g
9.8 m/s²
Initial vertical velocity, uy
0 m/s

02 Equations

Timet = √2h / g= √(20 / 9.8) = 1.43 s
RangeR = u × t= 2.0 × 1.43 = 2.86 m
Vertical speedvy = g × t= 9.8 × 1.43 = 14.00 m/s
